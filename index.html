About this dashboard

Wards are BBMP's 198-ward boundary. Sensor locations come from KH Projects. Water-level and discharge readings are the latest KH cleaned snapshot; clicking any ward isolates it so you can focus on its sensors.

Click any ward for its summary and sensor list; click a sensor to open its details and time-series charts.
